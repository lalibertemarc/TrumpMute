# TrumpMute

## v1.1.0

- "Republican" and "Republicans" now mute too, same whole-word rules as "trump" (not "Republicanism").
- The "muted" notice names the word they said.

## v1.0.0

- First release for WoW Forever.
- Anyone who says "trump" in chat is muted: that message and everything they say after is hidden from your chat windows.
- `/trumpmute` (or `/tmute`) to list, mute, unmute or clear players, turn it off, or mute silently.
